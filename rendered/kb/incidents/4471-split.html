The three problems

parser — the shared parser reads dates month first. Every tenant here writes the day first, so a date with a day of 12 or less swaps: 7 April goes out as 4 July. Covers app and email reports from every tenant, and form reports from booking-v2. The queued fix is for this one.

hint — the booking-v3 form asks for MM/DD/YYYY. Clients of day-first tenants type what the hint says, and the form reads it day first. The result looks like the parser bug and has a different fix: the hint. The #4471 page already placed these apart (“no ticket of its own”); now they have one.

one-day — dates move by one day. Not a swap: Tuesday 12 May shows as Monday 11 May; a Lisbon client gets Tuesday and a Toronto client Monday for the same meeting; an invite arrives before the booking was made. That points at time zones. The desk had already noted these as a one-day move; the #4471 page counts them under parser, where the parser fix won’t reach them. One reporter has stopped reporting it, so expect more than the export shows.
